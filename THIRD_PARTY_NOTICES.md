## Music Videos

The Music Videos route preserves Linacre's original player by embedding [LIN4CRE/linacre-music-videos](https://github.com/LIN4CRE/linacre-music-videos) from its official GitHub Pages origin. Ten cover-art files and a small track catalogue are served locally; the original video masters, subtitles, synchronized lyric engine and playback implementation remain with the source project rather than being duplicated into this repository.
