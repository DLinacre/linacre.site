What the badges mean

  • Browser — listed as a browser game; the external host determines availability and requirements.
  • Online — multiplayer or online rooms; some also play solo.
  • External sites may change, require files, or be unavailable. Check the host before playing.

Quick tips

  • Mouse look: click the game to capture the mouse, Esc to release.
  • No sound? Click or press a key once — browsers block autoplay.
  • Saves live in your browser; clearing site data deletes them.
  • Big ports (WebGPU / large downloads) work best in desktop Chrome or Edge.
