      HELIX

      A population of tiny programs is already alive in this page. Each coloured light is an organism. The double helix is whoever is winning.

      They compete, cross, and mutate. When the crowd becomes too alike the universe collapses — and is reborn from what already worked. Nothing is uploaded. You can sit and watch, or steer it from the bar below.
